# Present-Day Prep and Zombie Drill Shelves

Run `scripts/download_prep_security_library.sh` to verify and install the eight official PDFs.
The downloader rejects non-PDF responses, verifies page counts, extracts local reader text, and
records SHA-256, source URL, publisher, safety note, and rights note in
`library/prep-security-provenance.tsv`.

“Zombie” is a fictional drill label. Its shelves contain real shelter-in-place, wind retrofit,
safe-room, and defensive risk-assessment references useful for storms, chemical releases, civil
disorder, and property protection. They are not tactical or anti-personnel instructions.

The arms shelf is limited to lawful firearm and ammunition safety, secure storage, theft
prevention, and inventory records. It does not provide concealment or evasion guidance.
