WAYKEEPER (TM) HARDWARE SELF-REPAIR MANUAL                                CARD 1 OF 10
================================================================================
SCOPE
  Concept-level field service for the Orange Pi Zero 2 WayKeeper architecture:
  enclosure, display/touch, storage, USB peripherals, radios, cooling, connectors,
  and replaceable power modules. The final BOM and board revision always control.

+----------------------------- STOP FIRST ---------------------------------+
| Shut down cleanly. Disconnect solar/external power. Isolate the battery using |
| the designed service disconnect or remove the approved battery module. Wait.  |
| Work dry, with ESD precautions, and photograph every connector before removal.|
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

EVACUATE / ISOLATE - DO NOT FIELD REPAIR
  Swollen, punctured, leaking, wet, smoking, hissing, unusually hot, or sweet-
  smelling battery; burned charger/BMS; melted wire; active corrosion; arcing.
  Move people away, follow local battery/fire guidance, and use trained service.

NO LIVE RESISTANCE OR CONTINUITY TESTS. NO BMS BYPASS. NO LARGER FUSE.

SAFE MODULE PROCEDURES

ENCLOSURE / STRAP / GASKET
  Remove power. Photograph routing. Replace cracked shell, strap, or exact gasket.
  Hand-start fasteners; use the controlled torque pattern. Pinched gaskets, cables,
  and overtightened inserts destroy ingress protection. Never claim a field-opened
  unit is waterproof; perform the approved seal check before exposure.

DISPLAY / TOUCH
  Confirm the system boots through UART or an external display first. Power off
  and isolate battery. Release the connector latch correctly; pull the stiffener,
  not the flex. Reseat once, square and fully inserted. Replace torn or creased
  flex cables; do not bridge them with foil or field solder.

STORAGE / BOOT
  Preserve the original microSD. Boot a verified WayKeeper recovery image on a
  known-good card. If recovery succeeds, copy only inspected state/data. Do not
  copy damaged system files blindly. Record image hash and card identity.

RADIO, THERMAL, AND INPUT SERVICE

WI-FI / BLUETOOTH
  Check STATUS, rfkill/service state, firmware, and the known-good image before
  opening the unit. With all power isolated, inspect approved antenna connectors.
  Press micro-coax connectors vertically with the correct tool; never twist or
  pry sideways. Do not alter antenna length, routing, shielding, or transmit power.

COOLING
  Stop the load if the enclosure is unusually hot. Remove dry dust with a soft
  brush while unpowered. Replace only with the exact pad thickness and placement;
  a thinner pad may not touch and a thicker pad may bend the board. Do not fill
  designed air gaps with paste. Confirm vents remain open after assembly.

BUTTONS / KEYBOARD / TOUCH INPUT
  Check software input enumeration first. Substitute one known-good USB HID device.
  Clean external keys dry. Replace a sealed input module rather than flooding it
  with cleaner. Verify no stuck key prevents the ESC-W-K-ENTER wake sequence.

POWER SYSTEM BOUNDARY

FIELD-REPLACEABLE ONLY WHEN THE FINAL BOM DEFINES THE MODULE
  * Approved external USB-C cable and source
  * Protected input daughterboard
  * Battery/UPS pack with matching chemistry, connector, BMS, and rating
  * Exact-rated fuse or fused module
  * Approved regulator or charger module

DO NOT FIELD-REPAIR
  * Battery cells, spot welds, protection PCB/BMS, charger, MPPT, or swollen pack
  * Burned/corroded multilayer PCB, cracked board, lifted USB-C power pads
  * Unknown fuse, polarity, connector pinout, or improvised cell substitution
  * Any modification that bypasses thermal sensing, cutoff, interlock, or BMS

USB-C LATER-RELEASE UART NOTE
  A USB-C connector does not make raw UART electrically compatible. Route future
  UART only through the documented USB device/bridge design. Never attach UART to
  USB D+/D-, CC, SBU, or VBUS based on connector shape or an unverified cable.

POST-REPAIR TEST - CASE OPEN, THEN CLOSED

  [ ] Inspect for loose hardware, conductive debris, pinched cable, reversed plug.
  [ ] Check resistance/continuity only while fully unpowered and as BOM directs.
  [ ] Use the approved source. Current limit only to a documented safe BOM value.
  [ ] First power briefly; watch current, heat, smell, smoke, display, and boot log.
  [ ] Stop immediately on abnormal current, heat, odor, sound, reset, or swelling.
  [ ] Boot known-good image; check storage, display, touch, keys, USB, radios, UART.
  [ ] Test battery and charge behavior under supervision; never leave it unattended.
  [ ] Shut down, close with correct cable routing, gasket, fasteners, and torque.
  [ ] Repeat functional test. Perform only the approved non-destructive seal check.
  [ ] Update journal with fault, cause, parts, image hash, tests, and remaining risk.

RETURN-TO-FIELD GATE
  The unit boots repeatedly, shuts down cleanly, charges normally without heat,
  holds all connectors, passes input/radio checks, and has no unresolved damage.
  Otherwise label it OUT OF SERVICE and preserve it for trained repair.
