# WAYKEEPER Agriculture and Horticulture Library

This is a compact, searchable starter library for food production and farm
continuity. It contains 26 validated PDFs arranged across 15 field shelves:
soil; seeds; vegetables; staple and calorie crops; orchards and nuts; water;
greenhouses and hydroponics; integrated pest management; tools; livestock;
pasture and feed; calendars and zones; New York planting and harvest;
post-harvest storage; and farm/community planning.

Run `scripts/download_agriculture_library.sh` to fetch or revalidate every
listed source, extract page-preserving text, update `library/catalog.tsv`, and
write SHA-256, byte, page, source, safety, and rights records to
`library/agriculture-provenance.tsv`.

The pack favors USDA, state agencies, Cooperative Extension, FAO, SARE, and
clearly identified nonprofit sources. A source being publicly readable does
not automatically make it redistributable. Rows marked for licensing review
are suitable for this personal offline unit but must be cleared, replaced, or
excluded before any commercial image is made.

Field conditions and law control. Soil contamination, pesticide labels,
veterinary care, animal welfare, structural loads, wells, potable water,
electrical work, food safety, zoning, and certification all require current
local information and qualified judgment.
