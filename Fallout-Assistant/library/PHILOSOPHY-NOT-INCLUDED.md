# Philosophy full text not included

Call and acquisition sheet for the 72 philosophy entries that are present in
the WAYKEEPER menu but do not currently contain the work's full text.

Current corpus status: **197 entries total / 125 full text / 27 license or
copyright required / 45 edition and rights review required**.

An additional 21 curated TEXTFILES.COM archive works are installed locally but
have not been cleared for redistribution; they are listed in Section C.
Three useful Federal Reserve barter/money publications found during the economy
search remain link-only pending document-specific reuse review; they are listed
in Section D. The included *A Yen to Trade* curriculum is not on that list
because its PDF expressly grants educational reprint/photocopy permission with
credit.

One local underground Cookbook PDF is preserved as a quarantined menu record
but excluded from Guide and DeepSearch; its rights and content status are listed
in Section E.

WAYKEEPER is currently a personal, offline, noncommercial archive. For each
licensed work, request written permission or a license to retain the complete
work locally, convert it to a paginated reader adaptation, index it for offline
search, and use short retrieved passages as evidence for the local assistant.
Record any limits on format conversion, number of devices, backups, excerpts,
future distribution, and duration. Buying an ebook does not automatically grant
those adaptation or redistribution rights.
