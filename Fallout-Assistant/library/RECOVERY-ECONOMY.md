# Survival economy and rebuilding society

The F9 Society tree now separates two practical collections:

- **Survival Economy** covers barter mechanics, voluntary exchange, the
  functions and characteristics of money, weights, measures, method of sale,
  packaging, pricing, fuel quality, and economic comparison of resilience
  investments.
- **Rebuilding Society** covers community recovery, continuity, lifelines,
  inclusive planning, infrastructure dependencies, and restoration priorities.

Run `scripts/download_recovery_economy_library.sh` to acquire and verify the
ten open or expressly reusable institutional PDFs. The importer checks the PDF signature and page
count, extracts layout-preserving page text, records SHA-256 provenance, and
merges the documents into the main reader catalog.

The Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis curriculum expressly permits educational
reprinting or photocopying with credit. Its barter exercises explain voluntary
exchange, coincidence of wants, value comparison, commodity money, trust, and
why a commonly accepted exchange medium can emerge; they do not prescribe a
currency or fixed post-disaster prices.

These sources do not create currency, suspend law, authorize taking property,
or make a proposed trade fair. Use voluntary agreement, honest measures,
transparent records, accessibility, and current local law. Alcohol production,
distribution, and sale remain regulated; beverage-spirit distillation requires
federal approval and can also violate state or local law.
