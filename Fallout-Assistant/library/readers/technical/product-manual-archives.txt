WAYKEEPER // PRODUCT MANUAL ARCHIVE INTELLIGENCE
CHECKED 2026-08-16

PURPOSE

Find exact operating, installation, parts, and service documentation without
trying to mirror the entire public Internet. Counts change continually. Free
online access does not automatically grant redistribution rights.

MANUALSLIB

Live site claim: 10,522,811 PDF manuals, 7,292,332 products, 141,039 brands,
and 3.2 TB of data indexed. This is the clearest published storage estimate.
A complete copy exceeds a 1 TB field unit and is more than twelve times a
256 GB card before filesystem overhead or a search index.

Source: https://www.manualslib.com/

INTERNET ARCHIVE MANUALS

The collection search returned 3,038,231 items. An item may contain an original
scan plus OCR, thumbnails, metadata, and other derivatives, so item count is not
equal to PDF count and does not reveal a trustworthy total byte size. A complete
mirror is not a practical WayKeeper target. Rights and reuse terms vary by item.

Source: https://archive.org/details/manuals

IFIXIT

The home page displayed 580,425 free step-by-step guides. iFixit states that its
content is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 3.0: attribution, noncommercial use, and
share-alike terms apply. This makes it attractive for a personal/offline repair
pack, but not automatically suitable for a future commercial WayKeeper product.

Sources:
https://www.ifixit.com/
https://about.ifixit.com/Info/Licensing

MANUALSONLINE AND MANUALS+

ManualsOnline advertises search over 700,000 products and thousands of free
manuals. Manuals+ is useful as a discovery layer for manufacturer manuals and
other repositories, but no reliable total archive size was located. Prefer the
original manufacturer's current PDF whenever possible.

Sources:
https://www.manualsonline.com/
https://manuals.plus/about-us

RECOMMENDED WAYKEEPER ARCHITECTURE

1. Keep a small catalog: manufacturer, model, year/revision, category, manual
   type, language, source URL, rights/license, SHA-256, local path, and date
   checked.
2. Cache only manuals for equipment actually owned plus high-value common tools,
   generators, radios, solar controllers, inverters, chargers, pumps, vehicles,
   and medical devices.
3. Prefer manufacturer sources, government/public-domain manuals, and clearly
   licensed repair guides.
4. Mark every manual with revision status. Safety bulletins and model-specific
   service information can supersede an older document.
5. Design storage tiers: a small field pack for 256 GB media and a larger personal
   archive for the 1 TB unit. Do not promise a universal offline mirror.

IMPLEMENTED DISCOVERY CONTROL

The F2 Technical Workshop now includes ONLINE MANUAL RESOURCES. It searches the
Internet Archive Manuals collection and iFixit's official guide API, creates a
direct ManualsLib provider search, and hands ManualsOnline to its own search
interface. Results may be staged as metadata for later review. There is no bulk
download control; source content remains untouched until exact model, revision,
rights, safety, storage, and checksum review is complete.

PRACTICAL CAPACITY ANSWER

A full ManualsLib-scale collection alone is stated as 3.2 TB, so it does not fit
on either target. A curated 10-25 GB core repair library plus model-specific local
manuals is realistic. Metadata-only indexes for millions of records can remain
compact, while PDF downloads are selective and checksum-verified.

SAFETY

Use the exact current model manual for torque specifications, lifting points,
load charts, lockout, battery isolation, high-voltage systems, refrigerants,
airbags, pressurized systems, and firmware/service procedures. A nearby manual
for a similar product is not an acceptable substitute.
