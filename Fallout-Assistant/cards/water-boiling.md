# Making water safer by boiling

If the concern is germs and bottled water is unavailable, filter cloudy water through a clean cloth or let it settle. Bring the clear water to a rolling boil for 1 minute. Above 6,500 feet (about 1,980 m), boil for 3 minutes. Let it cool and store it in clean, covered containers.

Boiling does not remove fuel, toxic chemicals, or radioactive material. Use another water source if those contaminants are known or suspected.
