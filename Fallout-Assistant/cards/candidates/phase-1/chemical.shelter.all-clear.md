# REMAIN SHELTERED UNTIL THE OFFICIAL ALL CLEAR

Keep monitoring radio or mobile alerts. Leave the safe room only after police,
fire, or other local officials announce the all clear, then follow their exit
and decontamination instructions.

## LIMITS

If the room develops an immediate life threat such as fire or structural
failure, move away from that danger and contact emergency services if possible.
