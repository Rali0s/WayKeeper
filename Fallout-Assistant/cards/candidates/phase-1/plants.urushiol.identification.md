# USE SEVERAL TRAITS TO IDENTIFY URUSHIOL PLANTS

Learn the local forms of poison ivy, poison oak, and poison sumac before field
work. Use growth form, leaflet arrangement, berries, season, and location rather
than relying on one rhyme or leaf edge.

## LIMITS

These plants vary across regions and seasons. Do not touch an unknown plant to
confirm an identification; use a regional field guide or trained identifier.
