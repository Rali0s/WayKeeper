# PACK CLOTHING AND WARMTH FOR EACH PERSON

Provide a sleeping bag or warm blanket for each person. Add a complete change
of clothing, including long sleeves, long pants, and sturdy shoes.

## LIMITS

Adjust insulation, rain protection, and spare layers for the local climate and
season. Keep clothing dry; wet fabric can rapidly reduce useful insulation.
