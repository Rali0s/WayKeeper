# PROTECT COPIES OF ESSENTIAL RECORDS

Place copies of identification, insurance policies, and important account
records in a portable waterproof container. Consider keeping modest emergency
cash and change with the kit.

## LIMITS

Protect sensitive records from theft and unnecessary disclosure. Do not store
the only copy of an essential document in a kit that can be lost or stolen.
