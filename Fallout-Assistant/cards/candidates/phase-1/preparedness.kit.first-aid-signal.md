# PACK FIRST AID AND A SIGNAL WHISTLE

Keep a stocked first-aid kit and a whistle that can signal for help. Place them
where they can be reached without unpacking the entire emergency kit.

## LIMITS

A kit does not replace training or professional care. Check seals and dates,
and use emergency services whenever they are available for serious injury.
