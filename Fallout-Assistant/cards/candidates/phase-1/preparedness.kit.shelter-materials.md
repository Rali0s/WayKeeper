# PACK MATERIALS FOR TEMPORARY SHELTER SEALING

Keep plastic sheeting, duct tape, and a dust mask in the emergency kit so the
household has basic materials when officials order sheltering in place.

## LIMITS

Use these materials only as directed for the actual hazard. An ordinary dust
mask does not protect against chemical gases or make contaminated air safe.
