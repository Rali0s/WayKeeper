# NEVER BURN SUSPECTED URUSHIOL PLANTS

Do not burn poison ivy, poison oak, poison sumac, or brush that may contain
them. Smoke can carry urushiol particles to skin, eyes, and the respiratory
tract.

## LIMITS

Move away from the smoke. Difficulty breathing, throat or facial swelling, or a
severe reaction requires emergency medical help.
