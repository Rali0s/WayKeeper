# PLAN FOR MEDICATION AND HOUSEHOLD NEEDS

Customize the kit with prescription medication, glasses, infant supplies, and
pet food, water, and equipment needed by the actual household.

## LIMITS

Follow prescription and storage labels. Rotate dated supplies, protect them
from temperature extremes, and arrange refills with a clinician or pharmacist.
