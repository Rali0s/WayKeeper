# KEEP SUPPLIES WHERE YOU SPEND TIME

Keep a full emergency kit at home and consider smaller portable kits at work,
in a vehicle, or in other places where you regularly spend time.

## LIMITS

Heat, freezing, moisture, and theft can damage vehicle supplies. Inspect each
kit separately and never leave temperature-sensitive medicine in a vehicle
unless its label permits that storage.
