# SEAL THE SELECTED ROOM FROM OUTSIDE AIR

Enter the safe room and shut its door. If supplies are ready, tape plastic over
windows, close and tape vents, and seal around the door. Use towels or clothing
at vents and under the door when plastic is unavailable.

## LIMITS

Do not delay reaching shelter to search for supplies. Continue monitoring
official alerts; a sealed room is a temporary protective action.
