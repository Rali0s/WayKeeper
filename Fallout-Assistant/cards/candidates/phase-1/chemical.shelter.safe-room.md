# CHOOSE A CHEMICAL-EMERGENCY SAFE ROOM

Preselect a large interior room that has few or no windows, can be sealed from
outside air, and has water if possible. For an outdoor chemical release, choose
the highest practical floor.

## LIMITS

This placement is specific to many chemical releases. Tornado and radiological
shelter locations differ. Follow officials if they specify another location.
