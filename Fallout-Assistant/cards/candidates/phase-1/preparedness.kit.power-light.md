# PACK BACKUP PHONE POWER AND LIGHT

Add an extra phone battery or charger, a flashlight, and compatible spare
batteries to the emergency kit. Test rechargeable items during routine kit
checks.

## LIMITS

Do not mix damaged, leaking, or incompatible cells. A phone or flashlight is
not a substitute for an independent warning radio when networks are down.
