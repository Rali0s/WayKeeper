# DO NOT RELY ON A VEHICLE AS CHEMICAL SHELTER

Cars and trucks may not be airtight enough to protect against an outdoor
chemical release. Use alerts and official instructions to locate the nearest
suitable shelter.

## LIMITS

Do not drive through a suspected release to reach a preferred building. The
correct movement depends on the release location, wind, routes, and officials.
