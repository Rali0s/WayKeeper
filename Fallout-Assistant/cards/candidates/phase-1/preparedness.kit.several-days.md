# STOCK SEVERAL DAYS OF WATER AND FOOD

Keep water and nonperishable food for several days in the basic emergency kit.
Choose food that household members can eat and that can be used without normal
refrigeration or cooking.

## LIMITS

This checklist does not set one quantity for every household. Account for local
hazards, climate, medical needs, children, and pets. Replace damaged or expired
supplies.
