# COVER SKIN AROUND POISONOUS PLANTS

When working near poison ivy, poison oak, or poison sumac, wear long pants,
boots, a long-sleeved shirt, and gloves. Avoid touching the face and eyes.

## LIMITS

Protective clothing can carry urushiol after the task. Remove it carefully and
wash it separately before reuse.
