# LET OFFICIALS DECIDE EVACUATE OR SHELTER

During a chemical emergency, monitor radio, television, mobile alerts, or text
alerts. Follow police, fire, and local emergency instructions on whether to
evacuate or shelter in place.

## LIMITS

Do not assume sheltering is always safer. If the release is inside your building
and you were exposed, current CDC guidance may direct you outside to fresh air.
