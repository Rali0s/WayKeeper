# ESCALATE A SEVERE POISONOUS-PLANT REACTION

Seek professional care for a severe or widespread reaction or a rash involving
the face or genitals. Call emergency services for difficulty breathing, throat
or facial swelling, or another severe allergic reaction.

## LIMITS

Do not rely on a field card to diagnose the exposure. Smoke inhalation and rapid
swelling can become emergencies even when little rash is visible.
