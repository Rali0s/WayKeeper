# WASH SKIN IMMEDIATELY AFTER PLANT EXPOSURE

Immediately rinse exposed skin with rubbing alcohol, poison-plant wash, or a
degreasing soap or detergent and plenty of water. Clean under the fingernails.

## LIMITS

Rinse often so wash liquid does not dry on the skin and spread residue. This is
decontamination, not treatment for breathing trouble or a severe reaction.
