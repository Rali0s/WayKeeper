# POISON PLANT BLISTER FLUID IS NOT CONTAGIOUS

Fluid from poison ivy, poison oak, or poison sumac blisters does not contain
urushiol and does not spread the rash. Remaining oil on skin, clothing, tools,
or other objects can still expose another area or person.

## LIMITS

Do not intentionally open blisters. Keep the area clean and seek medical advice
for infection, extensive rash, facial or genital involvement, or severe symptoms.
