# RECOGNIZE DIRECT INDIRECT AND SMOKE EXPOSURE

Urushiol exposure can come from touching a plant, touching contaminated tools,
clothing, equipment, or animal fur, or inhaling particles from burning plants.

## LIMITS

A plant may still contaminate gear after field work. Do not assume that avoiding
visible leaves prevented exposure, and do not burn unidentified brush.
