# DECONTAMINATE CLOTHING TOOLS AND GEAR

Wear disposable gloves while cleaning exposed tools, boots, and equipment.
Wash exposed clothing separately in hot water with detergent, and clean tools
with rubbing alcohol or soap and water.

## LIMITS

Avoid transferring residue to skin, household surfaces, or other laundry.
Discard disposable gloves after cleaning and wash exposed skin promptly.
