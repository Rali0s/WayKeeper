# STOP OUTSIDE AIR DURING CHEMICAL SHELTERING

When officials order chemical sheltering, close and lock outside doors and
windows. Turn off fans, air conditioning, and forced-air heat, and close the
fireplace damper.

## LIMITS

Apply this only to an outdoor chemical release and only while sheltering is
directed. Never disable equipment when doing so creates another immediate danger.
