# PREPARE A ROOM-SEALING SUPPLY PACK

Store duct tape, scissors, towels, and plastic sheeting with the safe-room kit.
Precut and label plastic for the room's windows so it can be installed quickly.

## LIMITS

Sealing is temporary and must follow an actual shelter order. These materials do
not remove contamination or make every room safe for an unlimited time.
