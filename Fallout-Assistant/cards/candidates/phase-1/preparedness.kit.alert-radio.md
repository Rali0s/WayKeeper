# KEEP AN INDEPENDENT EMERGENCY RADIO

Pack a battery-powered or hand-crank radio that receives NOAA Weather Radio
tone alerts. Include the correct spare batteries when the radio uses them.

## LIMITS

Confirm local reception before an emergency. Radio alerts supplement, but do
not override, instructions issued by local emergency authorities.
