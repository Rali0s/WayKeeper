# ADD BASIC EMERGENCY SANITATION SUPPLIES

Pack moist towelettes, sturdy garbage bags, and plastic ties for temporary
personal sanitation when normal water or waste services are unavailable.

## LIMITS

Keep waste away from food and water. Follow public-health and local disposal
instructions; sealed bags are temporary containment, not permanent disposal.
