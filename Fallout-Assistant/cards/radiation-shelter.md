# Immediate radiation-emergency shelter

Get inside, stay inside, and stay tuned to emergency officials. Prefer a basement or the middle of a substantial building, away from exterior walls, doors, windows, and the roof. Close and lock windows and doors.

If you were outside, remove the outer layer of clothing carefully and wash exposed skin if possible. Do not leave shelter until officials say it is safe unless the building is unstable or you have a life-threatening condition.
