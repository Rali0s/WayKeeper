# How long should water-purification tablets remain in the water?

Use the exact dose and contact time printed on the product label. There is no safe universal tablet time.

Ask for:

- brand and active ingredient;
- tablet strength and number of tablets;
- water volume;
- label directions and expiration;
- whether the water is cold, cloudy, or visibly contaminated.

Do not identify an unknown tablet by appearance. Chlorine or iodine may not reliably kill Cryptosporidium. Correctly used chlorine dioxide can require several hours. No tablet makes water contaminated with fuel, toxic chemicals, or radioactive material safe.

Example times such as 30 minutes apply only to the specifically labeled product and conditions. They are not a substitute for the package instructions.
