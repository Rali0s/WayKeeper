# RES Book Classification

This is the source-library taxonomy. Files remain in RES as preservation
masters; copies converted or indexed for WayKeeper live under
`Fallout-Assistant/library/`.

## Primary additions

- `Privacy & Surveillance/` - surveillance history, civil liberties, privacy
  defense, obfuscation, and operational security.
- `Fiction & Occult/H.P. Lovecraft/` - three literary volumes. They are
  classified as fiction first and occult literature second; they are not
  presented as spiritual instruction.
- `Archives/Agriculture/` - the cannabis and poppy cultivation volumes. Both
  are deliberately hidden from the normal agriculture shelves and normal
  WayKeeper search. The archive preserves them without treating them as routine
  field guidance.

## Legacy collection map

- `Occult/` - ceremonial magic, grimoires, esoteric history, and occult
  spirituality.
- `Crowley/Occult/` - author-focused duplicate preservation set. Exact-byte
  duplicates also exist in `Occult/`; they have not been deleted.
- `Chaos Magick/` - modern occult practice and chaos-magick spirituality.
- `Dark Tetrad/` - psychology, personality, manipulation, and behavioral-risk
  studies; not spirituality.
- `Memory/` - memory and cognition research. The duplicated *Red Dragon and
  Black Hen* scan is occult/grimoire material despite its legacy folder.

## Classification rule

Subject labels describe the material; they do not endorse factual, medical,
legal, historical, or spiritual claims. Fiction/occult overlap is kept visible
rather than forcing those books into a single doctrinal shelf.
