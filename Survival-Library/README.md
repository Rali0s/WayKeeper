# Extreme Survival Library

Downloaded and checked on 2026-08-15. This folder contains only government, public-domain, author-authorized, or openly licensed PDFs. Commercial books are linked below rather than copied.

## Start here

1. **Build a plan:** `Free-PDFs/FEMA-Are-You-Ready-Citizen-Preparedness.pdf`
2. **Learn field survival:** `Free-PDFs/ATP-3-50.21-US-Army-Survival-2018.pdf`
3. **Train for medical emergencies:** `Free-PDFs/WHO-ICRC-Basic-Emergency-Care-2018.pdf`
4. **Keep the existing deep SERE reference:** `../Counter-Intel/afh10-644.pdf`
5. **Prepare for special hazards:** use the nuclear and safe-room books only after the all-hazards basics are covered.
6. **Build long-term food resilience:** practice the USDA canning procedures while equipment, power, and expert help are available.

Books are references, not substitutes for hands-on training. Take CPR/AED, bleeding-control, wilderness first-aid, navigation, fire-building, shelter, and water-treatment training before an emergency. Do not improvise medical procedures, plant identification, canning times, or structural designs from memory.

### Important limits

- `AFH 10-644` and `ATP 3-50.21` are military references built around isolated-personnel scenarios. Civilian emergency planning, medicine, sanitation, and local hazards still need separate coverage.
- The WHO/ICRC workbook is training material for health workers. It does not authorize untrained invasive care or replace professional help.
- *Nuclear War Survival Skills* is useful but old. Use current government alerts and instructions during an actual radiological emergency. Its publisher specifically warns that some electronic-edition Kearny fallout-meter templates and illustrations are not fully reliable; obtain verified full-size templates before depending on them.
- FEMA P-320 plans have design limits. Local code, site conditions, permits, and a qualified design professional control any real construction.
- Canning errors can cause fatal botulism. Follow the tested recipe, jar size, pressure/time, and altitude adjustment exactly; never use an improvised process.
- Never eat a wild plant from a general survival-book description. Use a color, region-specific field guide, verify every diagnostic feature, and learn locally from an expert.

## Best commercial additions

Buy physical copies for the most important material; an offline library is still vulnerable to battery, screen, and device failure.

1. [NOLS Wilderness Medicine, 7th Edition](https://store.nols.edu/products/nols-wilderness-medicine-7th-edition) — best first purchase for assessment, evacuation decisions, trauma, and environmental illness. Pair it with a Wilderness First Aid or Wilderness First Responder course.
2. [The Survival Handbook, Colin Towell (2020)](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/541795/the-survival-handbook-by-colin-towell/) — highly visual, broad land/sea survival manual by a former military survival instructor; easier to use than many dense military publications.
3. [Where There Is No Doctor, 2nd Edition, 21st revised printing (2025)](https://store.hesperian.org/products/where-there-is-no-doctor) and [Where There Is No Dentist, 21st revised printing (2024)](https://store.hesperian.org/products/where-there-is-no-dentist) — long-duration, low-resource health references. Use within your training and local law.
4. [Sam Thayer's Field Guide to Edible Wild Plants of Eastern and Central North America](https://www.foragersharvest.com/sams-books.html) — the regional color guide to choose if this library is for the eastern/central U.S.; substitute a rigorous guide for the actual operating region.
5. [Mountaineering: The Freedom of the Hills, 10th Edition (2024)](https://www.mountaineers.org/books/books/mountaineering-the-freedom-of-the-hills-10th-edition) — knots, navigation, expedition planning, snow, glacier travel, rescue systems, and mountain risk.
6. [Deep Survival, Laurence Gonzales](https://wwnorton.co.uk/books/9780393353716-deep-survival-a0c51003-55d4-400e-a17f-f88a15c2928d) — judgment, stress, and why capable people make fatal decisions; valuable because survival is as much decision-making as technique.
